> HWP BWP · V.6.3 Inhaltliche Anforderungen · Rz 112 · S. 437–481

An agreed-upon procedures en
gagement may include specific
procedures to examine individual
items of financial information (for
example, an entity’s payables,
r eceivables, related party transac
tions, and segment revenues
and profits), a financial statement
(for example, a balance sheet),
or a complete set of financial
statements.

Agreed-upon procedures may
include inquiries, analyses, recal
culations, comparisons, obser
vations, inspections, other purely
formal checks and obtaining of
confirmations. However, no overall
assessment is made and respec
tively, no audit opinion/audit
statement is expressed.

In an agreed-upon procedures en
gagement, the practitioner carries
out those examination procedures
of an audit nature to which the
practitioner and the entity and any
appropriate third parties have
agreed, with the objective to
r eport on factual findings. The
persons defining the procedures
or agreeing those with the prac
titioner, may be representatives of
the entity or third parties.
