> HWP BWP · V.6.3 Inhaltliche Anforderungen · Rz 112 · S. 437–481

If the examination involves an
audit of financial statements or
other historical financial informa
tion with the objective of obtaining
reasonable assurance, this audit
shall be conducted in considera
tion of the respective engage
ment’s circumstances and the
r equirements of the Swiss Stand
ards on Auditing (SA- CH), unless
otherwise required by law or other
legal requirements.
