> HWP BWP · V.6.3 Inhaltliche Anforderungen · Rz 112 · S. 437–481

Ordinary audit
Ordinary audits are governed by
art. 728 et seq. CO. An ordinary
audit is performed by the statutory
auditor, appointed as per the regu
lations of the CO. The statutory
auditor has to determine whether
the annual financial statements
and, if applicable, the group finan
cial statements comply with Swiss
law, the articles of incorporation
and the applicable framework,
whether the proposal of the board
of directors to the general meet
ing of shareholders regarding the
appropriation of available earn
ings complies with Swiss law and
the articles of incorporation, and
whether an internal control sys
tem is in place. An ordinary audit
is an «assurance engagement», i. e.

an engagement in which a practi
tioner expresses a conclusion
about the outcome of the evalua
tion or measurement of a subject
matter against criteria. The nature
and purpose of the engagement
are described in the Swiss Stand
ards on Auditing (SA- CH). In the
auditor’s report on the ordinary
audit the practitioner provides
reasonable assurance.
