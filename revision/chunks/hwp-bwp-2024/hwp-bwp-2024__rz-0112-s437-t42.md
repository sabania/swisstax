> HWP BWP · V.6.3 Inhaltliche Anforderungen · Rz 112 · S. 437–481

Audit
Audits primarily enable the auditor
to express a conclusion about the
outcome of the evaluation or
measurement of a subject matter
against criteria. The purpose of
such a conclusion is to enhance
the degree of confidence of the
intended users who are not identi
cal with the persons responsible
for the presentation of the subject
matter.

Prüfungsgegenstand
Der Prüfungsgegenstand sind die
der Prüfung zugrunde liegenden
Sachverhaltsinformationen.

Objet de l’audit
L’objet de l’audit est le contrôle
des informations factuelles sousjacentes à l’audit.

Scope of audit
The scope of the audit is the sub
ject matter information to be
examined against suitable criteria.
